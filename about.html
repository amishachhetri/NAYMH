About NAYMH

Championing Mental Health, Breaking Stigma

Our Mission

New American Youth Mental Health (NAYMH) is dedicated to creating a world where mental health is championed, stigma is shattered, and hope is restored. We are a group of passionate young adults committed to addressing the unique mental health challenges faced by New American youth.

Our Vision

We envision a future where every New American youth has access to culturally sensitive mental health resources, a supportive community, and the tools they need to thrive. We believe in breaking down the barriers that prevent young people from seeking help and creating spaces where vulnerability is met with compassion.

Our Values

Compassion

We approach every individual with empathy and understanding

Accessibility

Mental health resources should be available to everyone

Community

Together, we are stronger and more resilient

"A World Where Mental Health is Championed, Stigma Shattered, and Hope Restored."
