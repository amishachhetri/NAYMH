Learn more about NAYMH and how you can be part of our community

New American Youth Mental Health (NAYMH) builds community for New Americans through youth-led spaces that center lived experiences, foster learning, and advocate for equitable access to mental health care.

We create safe, supportive environments where young people can share their stories, learn from one another, and work together to break down barriers to mental health support in immigrant and refugee communities.

New American Youth Mental Health (NAYMH) serves individuals and families from all backgrounds, with a particular focus on New Americans — especially those from immigrant and refugee communities.

Our work is youth-led and community-driven, creating spaces where young leaders shape conversations around mental health, identity, and belonging. While we center the experiences of New Americans, our programs are open to anyone seeking culturally responsive support, connection, and advocacy.

You can get involved with NAYMH by volunteering your time, attending our events and workshops, supporting our programs through donations, or helping spread awareness about our mission. Every action — big or small — helps us build stronger, more connected communities.

Here are some ways to connect with us:

  • Attend our events: Join our conferences, workshops, and community gatherings
  • Volunteer: Share your skills and time to support our programs
  • Spread the word: Follow us on social media and help raise awareness
  • Share your story: Your experience could inspire and help others
